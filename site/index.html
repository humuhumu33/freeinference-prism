freeinference

Free verified AI inference.

An open source runtime that verifies its own answers and makes computation reusable across machines, without running it again.

No account, no key, no price per token. It runs on your own GPU, in this browser, and keeps working offline.

BitNet 2B 4T, ternary, 0.69 GB, verified block by block

01Every weight is content addressed and verified before use.

02Every answer is sealed under its own address and can be re derived on your device.

03A repeated prompt is served from its receipt, nothing runs again.
